# Relay

## Narration

Employee service portals make people hunt for the right system, record, owner, and form before they can solve a simple problem. Relay begins with the employee's words. It reads authenticated workspace records, opens the exact task or document, and prepares consequential actions for review. A VPN issue becomes a complete IT Support request; the employee sees the destination and details, confirms the action, and receives a verified case record. The evaluation covered seven workflow families, held-out rewordings, voice sessions, safety scenarios, and replay testing. After the fix pass, Relay completed 103 of 110 task-success conversations and 39 of 42 held-out requests. Its defining achievement is the shared execution contract: identity, approval, idempotency, audit history, and postcondition checks work the same way across every capability.
