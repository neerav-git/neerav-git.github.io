# Refract

## Narration

Research arguments are assembled across PDF readers, AI chats, notebooks, and spreadsheets, and each transfer can detach a claim from its source. Refract makes one persistent research session the continuity layer. In the Reader, a selected passage can be explained, summarised, quizzed, or saved, and every grounded answer keeps its page and chunk. Index and Compare turn that evidence into concept memory, cross-paper comparisons, and a coverage map that exposes gaps before writing begins. A React front end calls a Fast API service, with Postgres for sessions, Chroma DB for retrieval, and Claude for grounded synthesis. Stats moves a dataset through ordered checkpoints: profile, audit, and baseline regression. Each column receives an analytical role, and Spearman screening flags leakage and redundancy before any model trains. In a battery remaining-useful-life case study, cycle index was flagged as leakage and excluded through a saved decision, and models were compared on a fixed split and seed. Interpretive claims carry one of five provenance labels, and anything unlabelled stays unresolved. What makes Refract distinctive is the preserved chain: paper, passage, question, dataset, feature decision, model, and metric stay connected in one session.
