# Brain Atrophy Analysis in Dementia

## Narration

Measuring brain atrophy from MRI can require expert tracing and time-consuming segmentation. This study asked whether visible tissue loss, ventricular expansion, and widened sulci could become a repeatable image-level measure. The OpenCV workflow separates dark space, bright tissue, and mid-range tissue, then computes an auditable ratio: black pixels divided by white plus grey pixels. Across 2,198 ADNI T1-weighted MRI images, cohort averages established lower and upper reference bounds of 0.41 and 0.83. The same method was evaluated with two anatomical views. Focusing the crop around the hippocampal region improved reported accuracy from 66 to 71 percent. The work is distinctive because the anatomical hypothesis, pixel masks, equation, reference bounds, and crop comparison remain visible from input image to result, creating an interpretable computer-vision pipeline that was published in the IEEE ICRITO 2022 proceedings.
