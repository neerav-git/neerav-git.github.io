# Birb Hangout

## Narration

Most remote social products demand a conversation or a shared game. Birb Hangout was built for the quieter rituals that make physical presence comfortable: reading while a friend chooses music, resting by the fire, watching together, sharing a hug, or taking flight. Friends enter a private 3D lounge as birds, and the room itself becomes the interface. Records, books, television, trivia, gestures, and furniture make activities visible and easy to join. Resting by the fire opens into a shared Dream where momentum-driven flight crosses rivers, forests, and floating islands. The engineering matches synchronization to each social behavior, from authoritative room state to peer voice and staged interactions. Its special contribution extends into development: specialist studios preserve camera, scene state, and spatial annotations so visual feedback becomes reproducible evidence for animation and world design.
