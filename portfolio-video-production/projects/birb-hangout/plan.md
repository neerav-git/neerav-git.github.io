# Birb Hangout: film plan

Runtime target 70–80 s · cut from the owner's full-resolution 60 fps two-player captures (`~/Desktop/birb hangout video captures for website/`, 1866–2060 px wide), not the 960 px site derivatives · the warm lounge palette

## Story: one evening in the lounge

Two friends arrive and drift between activities without needing a plan. They end by the fire, fall asleep together, and fly through the Dream. A closing beat shows the engineering and studio tooling behind the feel.

## Draft narration (≈150 words)

1. Distance takes away the easy moments: reading while a friend picks music, resting by the fire, watching something together.
2. Birb Hangout brings them back. Friends meet as birds in a private 3D lounge.
3. The room is the interface: a record cabinet for music, a shelf of real books, a television for shared viewing.
4. Trivia ends with the loser ejected into space.
5. A hug is one gesture away.
6. Resting by the fire, the birds fall asleep together, and the Dream opens.
7. Momentum-driven flight carries them over rivers, forests and floating islands.
8. Underneath, each behaviour gets its own synchronisation model: durable room state, relayed flight, peer-to-peer voice, shared media intent and timestamped ceremonies.
9. Specialist studios keep camera, scene state and spatial notes, so visual feedback becomes reproducible evidence.
