# TraceLens

## Narration

Health-information models can recognize a dangerous situation and still leave the reader with a delayed or hedged next step. TraceLens measures the action that reaches the user. A pinned chatbot answers the message; Vega reads the same message independently; and a validated recommendation reader extracts the care action from the response. When the two disagree, the flag is disclosed and the chatbot reconsiders. Every stage is stored separately, so detection, correction, and the final recommendation can each be evaluated. On held-out physician-labelled cases, Vega reached an AUC of 0.966 and caught 18 of the chatbot's 31 missed emergencies. The saved support replay raised emergency guidance from 39 to 47 of 70 with one additional false alarm among 134 non-emergencies. TraceLens is special because the metric, evidence receipt, preregistration, calibration, and blinded review live inside one reproducible evaluation workspace.
