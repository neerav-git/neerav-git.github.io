# Deep Strat — AI Research Intern, August to November 2023

## What Neerav did

Deep Strat was where Neerav first did forward-deployed AI work: he sat with the people who had the problem, turned it into a working AI system, and advised the company's leadership on AI safety and how to use AI responsibly.

He partnered with department leadership to understand how customer feedback and complaints actually moved through the company, then defined the requirements and built a three-stage LLM workflow around them. The workflow reads each customer message, works out its sentiment and what the customer wants, finds the department responsible, and writes a report that follows that team's standard operating procedures. Teams stopped receiving raw customer messages and started receiving structured cases they could review and act on.

He also improved the company's churn prediction. Evaluating Random Forest and convolutional models on 2,000 customer records against a cost-sensitive objective tied to operational profit, he reduced false churn alarms by 25 percent while keeping the model's overall accuracy at separating leaving and staying customers stable, measured as AUC. In practice the business stopped spending retention effort on customers who were never going to leave.

## AI policy and safety advisory

Neerav consulted the founders and policy researchers on AI policy, AI safety, guardrails, and the ways models fail. He authored a 50-page technical reference synthesizing AI-safety research, known model failure modes, the debates among leading researchers, and his own analysis, organized by recurring failure patterns, competing research positions, and open questions. He presented it to the company's founders, the COO, and the research assistants, and it supported jointly authored AI-safety research presented at the Nullcon security conference.

He also wrote a comprehensive internal manual for the firm that teaches the important AI concepts from traditional machine learning through large language models, including embeddings, attention, hallucination, bias, and reinforcement learning from human feedback, so the whole team could reason about AI with a shared vocabulary.

## Why it matters for a hiring manager

This role shows the combination Neerav brings to customer-facing AI work: he can talk with founders and department heads, turn their problem into requirements, build the AI system, measure it against a business objective, and advise leadership on safety and policy. It is direct evidence for forward deployed engineering, applied AI engineering, solutions engineering, and AI safety or policy-adjacent roles.
