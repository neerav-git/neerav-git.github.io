About

I take AI products from an ambiguous workflow problem to an implemented interface, controlled system behavior, and a result that can be inspected and evaluated.

My work spans product design, frontend and backend engineering, applied machine learning, system integration, and evaluation. I define the user journey and workflow boundaries, decide which actions belong to the interface or the agent, build the surrounding data and service contracts, and create evidence that shows what the system completed.

Across these systems, inputs, actions, and outcomes remain visible. Relay connects an employee question to authenticated records, the exact portal surface, confirmation-gated actions, and a verified application state. Refract connects papers, passages, comparisons, recall attempts, datasets, analysis decisions, and model runs through one persisted research session. Homeward turns discharge records into source-linked recovery tasks and coordinated review. Birb Hangout combines realtime rooms, synchronized media, reading, voice, games, consent-based social interaction, flight, and specialist 3D evaluation tools inside one shared world.

At Deep Strat I worked directly with the founders and department leadership. I built a three-stage LLM workflow that turned raw customer complaints into routed, SOP-grounded cases, reduced false churn alarms by 25% while keeping AUC stable, and consulted the founders and policy researchers on AI policy, safety, guardrails, and model failure modes. I wrote a 50-page AI-safety reference that supported jointly authored research presented at Nullcon, and an internal manual that teaches the firm's team AI concepts from classical machine learning through large language models, including embeddings, attention, hallucination, bias, and RLHF.

My toolkit spans PyTorch, TensorFlow, Keras, scikit-learn, and OpenCV for machine learning and computer vision; statistical modeling and hypothesis testing for research; and OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Hugging Face, Deepgram, and LangChain for LLM and voice systems, alongside TypeScript, React, Python, FastAPI, and SQL.

I also conduct applied ML research. The work here includes a peer-reviewed OpenCV method that converts MRI intensity regions into an interpretable atrophy score and a separate four-class TensorFlow study on ADNI brain MRI scans that improved held-out accuracy from 78% to 85% through probability-level ensembling.

What I built

  • Relay: a voice-first employee-service workspace evaluated across seven multi-turn workflow families, with 103/110 post-fix development conversations and 39/42 held-out rewordings completed.
  • Refract: five connected research workspaces for close reading, evidence capture, cross-paper synthesis, source-grounded recall, dataset auditing, and baseline modeling.
  • Homeward: the product direction, patient journey, interface, backend, synthetic data strategy, source-linked discharge workflow, specialist-agent architecture, controls, tests, and comprehensive documentation.
  • Birb Hangout: the product direction and complete client-server social world, including private rooms, customizable characters, shared media, reading, voice, trivia, consent-based interaction, Dream flight, procedural terrain, persistence, deployment controls, and visual-evaluation studios.
  • Medical imaging: a peer-reviewed 2,198-image atrophy-measurement study and a 6,400-image four-class CNN ensemble experiment.

How I work

  1. Define the exact human task, decision, and desired application state.
  2. Design the smallest coherent product journey that can complete the work.
  3. Keep sources, permissions, confirmations, and state transitions explicit.
  4. Build the interface, service contracts, persistence, and evaluation path as one system.
  5. Record the result with scenarios, metrics, artifacts, and visual review that another person can inspect.

This approach creates operating products with clear responsibility, observable behavior, and a practical path for improving performance.
