HOW TO READ IT
The workflow has two connected tracks

The literature track produces cited evidence; the analysis track produces audited datasets, model comparisons, and interpreted results.

The literature track ingests papers, supports passage-level reading, saves cited evidence, and compares claims across selected sources.

The analysis track uploads a dataset, profiles columns, checks data quality and leakage risk, compares models, and stores the resulting metrics. The session ID connects each result to the research goal and selected sources.

Reader outputs include highlights, notes, and cited passages. Compare consumes the selected papers and research goal. Stats consumes the uploaded dataset and stores profiles, audit checks, baseline results, model comparisons, and interpretation notes.
