BIBLIOGRAPHIC RECORD
Analyzing Brain Atrophy in Dementia Using Computer Vision

Neerav Chaudhary and Sourabh Singh Verma · 2022 10th International Conference on Reliability, Infocom Technologies and Optimization (ICRITO).

IEEE DOI 10.1109/ICRITO56286.2022.9964994 · proceedings pages 1234–1238.

RESEARCH SCOPE
Longitudinal MRI analysis and quantitative atrophy measurement

The paper studies structural brain change visible across MRI scans collected over time.

The study applies computer-vision methods to prepare longitudinal MRI images, extract structural information, and quantify change associated with brain atrophy. Its contribution is an image-analysis workflow and the measurements reported in the paper.

The later four-class CNN study has a different repository, dataset organization, prediction task, and evaluation record. Its evidence appears on a separate case-study page.
