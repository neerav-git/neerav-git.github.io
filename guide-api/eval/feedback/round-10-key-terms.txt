=== 6.4 (cat 6) route=/
Q: Tell me about Refract
A [107w]: Refract is a research workspace that keeps reading, evidence, recall, and analysis in one session. Its retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) setup means every answer points to the exact paper and page it came from, so a researcher can verify a claim in one step instead of rebuilding the trail across a PDF reader, chat tool, and spreadsheets. Learners answer and rate their confidence before seeing the source, so recall is measured honestly, and the Stats workspace pauses for a person to review data problems before any model result is accepted. Neerav designed and built the whole product, and its checks cover source-linked answers, recall-first review, and duplicate repair.
   show project.refract-ai — Refract: case study (Research and learning platform)
Q: What was hard about building it?
A [105w]: The hardest problems were about keeping the research chain intact. The first workspace model let the document and session state drift apart, so Neerav added a persisted session identity and recovery for older records, then used browser probes to confirm that navigation and reload kept the right source set. In Review, passive highlighting made it too easy to mistake recognition for recall, so the flow now asks for an answer and a confidence rating before revealing the source. In Stats, the risk of data leakage and premature model acceptance led to a staged audit with a human-review gate before any result enters the research record.
Q: Who would use it?
