## SRC.homeward-lessons · 3/5

**Q:** Homeward: lessons and next version (guide project record)

**Answer:** Three lessons from building Homeward carry directly into its next version.

Contract-first design is what made the safety model testable. Defining discharge review, next-step selection, source trust, role capabilities, approval, and failure behavior as explicit contracts, and sharing scenario functions between the domain tests and the evaluation runner, meant every guardrail case exercised the real product path. The next version starts from those contracts and the scenario matrix before any new interface work.

The deterministic paths are the foundation of the product. Provider rejection showed that plan navigation, lexical source search, saved-record summaries, citations, and care-team coordination keep a patient moving without any model, and the specialists add value on top of that baseline. Each new agent capability follows the same pattern: the model selects, and the application decides.

Uncertainty belongs in the data model. Missing dates, conflicting uploads, unreviewed imports, and open help requests became durable state with their own transitions, which is what let the product stay honest under pressure. Keeping that modeling discipline as the product grows is the core of its quality.

The path to launch changes the environment around the product: authenticated patients and care teams in place of a local care-team operator, real discharge records in place of fictional material and synthetic Synthea history, and clinical validation with care partners.

**Feedback:** contact first design, deterministic paths, etc, make the language direct and understandable as well as what impact or user experience  improvement  or workload automation it is linked to. Frame features in terms of how and end user is benefited from the methodology or approach.

**Q:** Walk me through how a Relay request goes from voice to a confirmed action.

**Answer:** Relay's voice guide turns a spoken need into a typed proposal, and the employee's explicit confirmation is what triggers the write. Speech is recognized by Deepgram and reasoned over by GPT-4.1, and the guide resolves the request against the signed-in employee's records. For a consequential request, the server prepares the action and the employee sees exactly what will happen. The confirmation token is bound to that employee, the action, its arguments, and the record revision. The write is idempotent, it leaves an audit event, and the app checks the visible result before reporting success.
